# 💅 Viral Varnish
### AI-Powered Trend Forecasting for Nail Artists

> **"Stop guessing what to paint. Start predicting what will sell."**

**Viral Varnish** is a predictive analytics engine designed for independent nail artists and salon owners. By analyzing social media trends, search volume, and historical sales data, it forecasts which nail design styles will generate the highest **Client Attraction Score** in the next 14 days.

---

## 🚀 The Business Goal

**The Problem:**
Nail trends move faster than ever. Artists waste hours practicing complex designs (like 3D Flowers or Chrome Ombre) that yield low engagement, while missing out on emerging "viral" styles that could fill their books.

**The Solution:**
Viral Varnish tells the artist **exactly what to practice, film, and promote** two weeks in advance.
